Assumptions — the arithmetic behind the numbers

  • Weights memory: FP16 ≈ 2 GB per billion parameters; INT8 halves it (1 GB/B); 4-bit quarters it (0.5 GB/B). Headroom for the KV cache comes from the 80 GB card, not from extra cards.
  • Session token rate: peak tokens/sec = concurrent sessions × turns × tokens/turn ÷ session length. A session's turns are spread over its average length, so this is the sustained generation rate the replicas must carry.
  • Interaction replicas: one replica (7–14B, INT8, one 80 GB GPU) sustains ≈ 2,000 generated tokens/sec. replicas = ceil(peak tokens/sec × (1 + headroom) ÷ replica rate), spread across the regional nodes.
  • Front layer — Sarvam open conversation: Sarvam (LLM 1) handles the open, free-form conversation with the customer. 30B mixture-of-experts at FP16 ≈ 60 GB of weights, served on 2 × 80 GB GPUs per regional node (Appendix A default). Speech (ASR/TTS) runs alongside it on the same node.
  • Scrutiny batch: case files are processed in a nightly window at ≈ 1,500 tokens/sec per GPU on long documents. GPUs = ceil(daily tokens ÷ (window × rate) × (1 + headroom)), held inside the Appendix A band of 2–4 GPUs.
  • Cost anchors (from §12 of the note): one 8 × 80 GB GPU server ≈ $150K–$200K; the $175K midpoint gives a unit GPU price of ≈ $21.9K per GPU, applied to each component's computed GPU count in the cost table below. CPU/host servers (gateway, guardrails, defender host, orchestration) are priced per server. Subtotal plus 20% for storage, network and backup.
